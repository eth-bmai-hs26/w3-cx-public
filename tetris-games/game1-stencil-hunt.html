Game 1 · Stencil Hunt

Day one on the quality line 🏭

Pixel Toys makes toys out of plastic parts shaped like Tetris pieces. A camera photographs every tray on the line. The photo is a grid: a dark square means plastic, a light square means empty.

👷
MARA · LINE SUPERVISOR
Welcome aboard! First job: I need to know how many T-parts are in each tray, and where they are. You can't pick the parts up. All you have is the photo and this see-through stencil.
Your stencil

Lay the stencil on the photo. If every coloured square of the stencil lands on plastic, you've found a T-part.

Slide the stencil over the tray

Task: Move your mouse over the tray to position the stencil. Click to write down how many of its 4 squares land on plastic. Find both T-parts: spots where all 4 are covered. Then let the machine check every remaining spot for you.
